Born-digital archives are any packages produced by the Digital Archives program.
These may also be referred to as Digital Archives, DigArch, DA, Electronic Records, and FA Components among other names.
Because they retain as much of the original creator's intent as possible, their content is notable compared to other packages for:

* the diversity in filename,
* the diversity in file formats, and
* the diversity in foldering depth

## Package Requirements

There are two general types of packages:

* Processed - files that have been selected, arranged, and described by processing archivists.
* Preprocessed - files that have been migrated from carriers in preparation for processing Processed Born-Digital Packages are.

At this time, only Processed digital archives are being ingested into Preservica.

### Processed Package Requirements

![alt text]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/DA_package_general.svg "Diagram showing the file and folder structure of a General
Born-Digital Archives Package")

1. The top-level folder must be named according to its ID within the processed archive, `M1234_ER_1`
2. The top-level folder must have two
folders, `metadata` and `objects`.
3. The top-level folder may contain an `access` folder if access representations must be pre-generated due to the complexity of the original format.

## Process

Born-digital archives are added to the ingest queue once the Digital Archives Team marks them as complete.
