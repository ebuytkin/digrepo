The following ingest process is under development.

Born-Digital Audio and Moving Image packages are any packages recorded and edited solely as digital files.
They are also referred to as BDAMI, OrigiDocs, TOFT, and Dance Oral History (DOH).
Their content is notable compared to other packages for:

* the similarities in form to Digitized AMI
* the diversity in technical details compared to Digitized AMI

## Package Requirements

1. The top-level folder must be named according to the SPEC Object ID assigned to the physical object, `1234567`
2. The bag payload must contain folders for `EditMasters` and `ServiceCopies`. Additional folders for `ArchiveOriginals`, `PreservationMasters`, and `Images` are not required.
3. Additional digitization logs and metadata may be stored in a `tags` folder at the root of the bag.

## Process

Born-digital AMI packages are added to the ingest queue once approved by the Digital Archives team.

Remediation work is performed by the Digital Preservation team.
