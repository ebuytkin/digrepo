Ingest processes should run on dedicated servers to provide optimal bandwidth for file transfers and uploads.
Servers might be specialized to a specific kind of ingest, such as born-digital archives or digitized audio and moving image.
Additional servers may be necessary to parallelize the workflow and increase bandwidth utilization or to support a new ingest process.
All ingest servers are managed by the Information Technology Group (ITG) and should be configured the same when possible.
