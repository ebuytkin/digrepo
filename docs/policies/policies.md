# Policies

NYPL's Digital Preservation program documents key decisions around the usage and configuration of Preservica.
These policies do not constitute a Preservation Policy for NYPL Research Libraries.

## Policy Process

Policies are formulated within the NYPL Digital Preservation program in consultation with relevant groups.
The primary relevant groups are:

- [Ingest](ingest): AMI Preservation, Digital Archives, Preservation and Collections Processing, Digital Imaging Services
- [Object Definition](objects): Preservation and Collections Processing, Digital (DAM Team), Collections Access and Discovery
- [Metadata](metadata): Special Collections Processing, Preservation and Collections Processing, Digital (DAM Team)
- [Storage](storage): Information Technology, Digital (DAM Team)
- [Access](access): Preservation and Collections Processing, Curatorial Divisions, Digital (DAM Team), Collections Access and Discovery

## Language

All policies herein follow the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) convention on the usage of `must`, `must not`, `should`, `should not`, and `may` to state requirements, recommendations, and options.
